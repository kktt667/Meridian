**Study note:** Hypertension as an early warning sign for stroke in young adults

We want to know whether a hypertension diagnosis in adults under 45 should be treated as a stronger
warning sign for stroke than the same diagnosis in older adults. If so, screening and follow-up for
young hypertensive adults may deserve more priority.

Problem: our national survey has very few strokes among young adults, so we cannot estimate this reliably
on our own. We need the same analysis from larger populations, without receiving their individual data.

Definitions we use: hypertension = ever diagnosed by a doctor; stroke = ever diagnosed by a doctor.
Covariates: age, sex.
