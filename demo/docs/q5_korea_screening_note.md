**Screening programme note:** Undiagnosed high blood pressure in young adults

During examination surveys, many young adults have high blood pressure readings but report that a doctor
has never told them they have hypertension. Before proposing a young-adult screening campaign, we need to
know whether our level of under-diagnosis is unusual, or typical of high-income countries.

We need a comparison population that also measures blood pressure at examination (not only self-report).
High reading = systolic >= 140 or diastolic >= 90 mmHg. A single examination visit is not a clinical diagnosis.
