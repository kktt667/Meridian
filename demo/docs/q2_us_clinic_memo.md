**From:** Cardiovascular prevention working group, a US primary-care network
**Re:** Should our prevention pathway differ for East Asian patients?

Our clinicians have noticed that several East Asian patients presented with stroke rather than heart attack.
National guidelines are largely built on populations that are mostly White. We are considering a
"stroke-first" prevention emphasis (tighter blood-pressure targets, salt counselling) for East Asian patients.

Constraints:
- In our national survey data, "Asian" is one broad category (Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Filipino, Vietnamese, South Asian ...).
- We cannot access data from Asian countries directly, and we must not receive individual patient records.
- Any change to the pathway needs evidence a clinical governance committee can review.

Hypothesis: East Asian patients' cardiovascular events are more often strokes than those of White patients.
